Eden Island

A Private Beach Escape on the Red Sea

Sail out to Eden Island, a private beach retreat just off the Hurghada coast, for a relaxed day of swimming, sunbathing and snorkeling in calm turquoise water. The trip includes round-trip boat transport, a sun lounger on the beach and free time to explore the island's coral shallows before heading back to the marina.
