A Private Beach Escape on the Red Sea

Sail out to Aqua Trip Hurghada, a private beach retreat just off the Hurghada coast, for a relaxed day of swimming, sunbathing and snorkeling in calm turquoise water. The trip includes round-trip boat transport, a sun lounger on the beach and free time to explore the island's coral shallows before heading back to the marina.

For hotels located outside Hurghada, an additional transfer fee applies. Our transfer is safe and fast — licensed drivers, air-conditioned vehicles and a fixed pickup schedule get you straight from your hotel to the marina with no waiting around. It's also better value than grabbing a taxi or booking a ride through a ride-hailing app: one flat fee for the whole group, no surge pricing, no haggling, and a driver who already knows your hotel and your tour time.
