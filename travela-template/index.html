About Aqua Trip

Red Sea days, planned by people who live here

Aqua Trip Hurghada is a local team running boat trips, island excursions and desert safaris. We work directly with local boat owners and guides, so you get the real trip at a fair, fixed price.

Tell us your hotel and your date. We handle the pickup, the boat and the plan, and you just enjoy the water.
